Quick answers

SIROS AC1: questions buyers ask

How much does the SIROS AC1 cost?

From ₹63,000 with a 48V 32AH lead-acid battery (50 km/charge) up to ₹90,000 with a 60V 45AH lithium battery. The table above lists every option. Your dealer confirms the final on-road price.

How far does the SIROS AC1 go on one charge?

Between 50 and 120 km per charge depending on the battery: 50 km with 48V 32AH lead-acid, up to 120 km with 72V 45AH lead-acid, and 80 or 110 km with the lithium options. Actual range varies with load and riding conditions.

How long does charging take?

Lithium batteries charge in about 3–4 hours and lead-acid batteries in about 7–8 hours, as stated in the SIROS vehicle catalog.

What warranty comes with it?

3 years on lithium battery packs and 1 year on lead-acid packs, standard across the SIROS range.

Where can I see or test ride the AC1?

At SIROS dealers across Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh. Find your nearest dealer, or book a test ride on WhatsApp.

Can I buy it on EMI?

Yes. Finance is available through SIROS finance partners; see the EMI reference plans. The final plan depends on your eligibility.
